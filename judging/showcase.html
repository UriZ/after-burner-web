AFTER BURNER

The build as it stands

A browser rebuild of SEGA’s After Burner II (1987), measured against the arcade over 24 rounds. Currently 9.3/10 by a fresh-context judge each round.

Play it live: http://localhost:5173/  (or :5174)
Arrows steer · Z gun · X missile (hold to lock, release to fire) · Q/E throttle

Six of the beats: 7 combat biomes, both refuel mechanisms (runway + airborne tanker), the title. Captured from the live build at HEAD.
