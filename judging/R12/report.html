Round 12 · The rebuild, measured against the real thing

Look first. Measure second.

Eleven rounds measured this game to four decimal places while it quietly played itself. R12 throws that out: the round now opens by looking at ours beside the arcade and by an agent actually playing it. The first playthrough found the headline in one sitting — the missile never fires, the gun kills without you, and nothing you do has any stakes. Three lanes are fixing it now.
“When I slam the stick the world rolls hard and the desert sunset looks the business — for stretches this really is After Burner. But my missiles might as well not be on the plane, most of my gun kills happened to me, and when I lost my last life the game quietly handed me a fresh set — so nothing I do feels like it matters.”

The gaps

ranked by what breaks the illusion
  1. 01 BUG
    The missile is unreachable by a player

    Zero locks in 3 minutes of play — and zero when the TL tried deliberately, sight on a 6-enemy cluster, button held 250 ticks. The scripted demo fires salvos, so it’s a live-input bug, not tuning. Half of AB2’s verbs are missing as played.

  2. 02 FEEL
    Kills happen to you, not by you

    Aiming your best gets 11 kills; doing nothing gets 8. The gun resolves its hits at the muzzle, not at the sight, and the tracer is too thin to see — so the trigger feels disconnected from the kill.

  3. 03 CAREFUL
    The sight is caged near the nose

    Mid-screen targets can’t be aimed at without banking the whole plane. But R11 verified this envelope matches the arcade — so the fix is gunnery (gap 2), not a wider sight. Do not uncage it.

  4. 04 MISSING
    Stage 1 has no land

    Island coastlines, crags and surf scroll past in the arcade; ours is uniform sea. Ranked last for six rounds because no scalar complained — the loudest thing a returning player notices.

  5. 05 MISSING
    The game has no stakes

    Score climbs from the first tick with zero hits, and running out of lives silently refills to three. No game-over, no sting — nothing you do, good or bad, lands.

  6. 06 FEEL
    Nothing drives the camera

    The renderer rolls hard on a stick-slam — genuinely dramatic — but cruising and the attract pilot never use it, so the baseline reads level where the arcade’s horizon swings wildly.

Keep — do not regress

  • Stick-slam barrel roll and held bank are genuinely dramatic
  • Death starburst → tumble → READY reads arcade-true
  • Stage 2 sunset desert composition is excellent
  • Deaths felt fair — the hit was always on screen

Method change this round: absence outranks error outranks imprecision; perceptual tolerance bands close questions for good; the standing playtest is an agent with hands on the stick, not a human we wait on. Full write-up in judging/R12/method-retrospective.md.
