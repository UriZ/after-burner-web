# R12 opens with a method retrospective — why eleven rounds plateaued

**Date**: 2026-11-14. **Written by the TL session, from the record, before any R12 build work.**

## The symptom

| round | score | note |
|---|---|---|
| R1→R5 | 3 → 7.5 | fast, real convergence |
| R6 | 8 | |
| R8 | 8.5 | revoked in spirit by R9: the density gate had driven the build AWAY from the arcade |
| R9 | 7 | trend line retired; stills 9 / motion 4 |
| R10 | 7.5 | |
| R11 | 8 | |

**Net +0.5 in six rounds**, with one full regression bought by tuning to a defective gate.
Meanwhile the only human verdict on record (R5 era, the owner, after actually playing):
**"it's very bad, lots of work to be done"** — filed at STATUS.md line 636, never ranked,
never made a gap. A judge score of 8/10 coexisting with that sentence means the score is
measuring something other than the primary directive.

R12 itself stalled on the pad for a month: two judge scripts written mid-October, then nothing.

## The diagnosis — four coupled failures

### 1. Gaps are generated by measurement residuals, not by looking

A gap exists in this project when *a measurable quantity disagrees with an arcade column*.
Whole missing systems produce no residual, so they cannot rank. The record shows exactly
this: R5's gap list put **"still entirely absent: throttle, barrel roll, resupply stages,
canyon stages, the full 23-stage structure, carrier/islands"** at rank **11 of 11**, below
nine tuning items. Six rounds later the build has **3 stages of 23**, no resupply pass, no
bonus stage — while the lateral envelope was verified **to four significant figures**
(±9.8483 %W vs ±9.85). No player on earth can perceive the second; every player notices the
first. The selection function is inverted relative to the directive it serves.

### 2. The meta-work compounds and now dominates the output

- Game source: **20.7k lines**. Judging reports: **41k lines**. REFERENCE.md: **4.8k**.
  STATUS.md: **1.1k lines / ~38k tokens — it no longer fits in a single file read.**
- 43+ acceptance criteria retired or repaired; a standing criteria lane consumed one of
  four-to-six lanes **every round** from R5 on.
- R11 produced **eleven withdrawals**, and its headline findings are about instruments
  (an upscale bias, a null lever, a provenance tag) — true, valuable, and not the game.

The epistemology works — R11's surviving numbers held up under independent re-measurement
better than any round's. But it has hit hard diminishing returns: **each marginal unit of
measurement discipline now buys less screen fidelity than the same unit spent drawing,
building, or looking.**

### 3. The context tax is throttling the lanes

Every fresh-context lane and judge must ingest STATUS + REFERENCE + GAME_SPEC + the binding
rule set before touching anything. That required reading grows every round (six citation
fields, per-palette mask licences, attribution horizons…). Lanes spend their budget defending
figures against the ledger instead of making the game look right. This is the most likely
reason R12 never left the pad.

### 4. Precision far beyond perception, while perceptual facts go unmeasured

The loop litigates differences of 0.002 %W while, for three consecutive rounds, **the game
played itself** (blind-stick kill share 91 %) — the owner's named complaint. The one
instrument that catches gestalt failures in seconds — a human, or a judge looking at a
side-by-side — was never on the required path. "What nobody has judged yet: feel and fun"
has been in STATUS.md since R5, unaddressed, under its own heading.

## The fix — "look first, measure second"

The new round shape, binding from R12, now written into CLAUDE.md:

1. **Every round opens with eyes, not instruments.** Fixed scenario set (attract/title,
   stage-1 combat ≥20 s, death+respawn, stage transition), ours at deterministic 60 Hz,
   arcade from STAGE_MAP — composited side-by-side. The judge writes the top things that
   break the "this is AB2" illusion **in player language**. Only items from this list (or
   the owner's playtest) become gaps. Measurement then serves a chosen gap — it locates the
   constant — it never generates gaps.
2. **Absence outranks error; error outranks imprecision.** Standing priority: missing
   system > wrong-looking/behaving system > constant outside its band > constant inside its
   band (that last category is forbidden work). Every round ships at least one item from
   the missing-system backlog.
3. **Perceptual tolerance bands close questions permanently.** Every numeric target carries
   a stop-threshold — 1 native pixel, 1 frame at 60 Hz, or the band the arcade itself varies
   across (never tighter than ~10 %). Inside the band → CLOSED, re-litigation forbidden.
   No more four-significant-figure verification of anything.
4. **Meta is capped.** The standing criteria lane is retired. New binding rules only when a
   current top-3 gap demands one. Measurement/instrument work ≤ ~20 % of a round. No new
   instrument where crop-and-look answers the question. The defect ledger stays (it is
   genuinely good) but becomes reference material, off the required reading path.
5. **The reading diet shrinks.** STATUS.md ≤ 150 lines: state, top gaps, hard warnings,
   pointers. The full historical STATUS is archived, not deleted. A lane's brief is STATUS
   + its own gap + the specific reference crops — nothing else is required.
6. **A human plays it.** Owner playtest (2 minutes, five sentences) every round or two;
   those sentences outrank any scalar and seed the next gap list alongside the side-by-side.

What is deliberately kept: fresh-context judging, disjoint file ownership, deterministic
capture, "crop it and look", provenance on figures that do get published, and the judge
re-measuring shipped claims. Those parts demonstrably work.

## What would have been different under this method

- R5–R11 would have shipped stage variety, resupply, and enemy attack patterns instead of
  six rounds of criteria repair — because "it has 3 of 23 stages" tops any gestalt list.
- R8's density-gate regression could not have happened: a side-by-side look at a sky holding
  14 aircraft vs the arcade's mostly-empty sky fails in one glance, no census needed.
- The "game plays itself" complaint would have been rank-1 from the first owner playtest,
  not discovered by probe in R10–R11.
